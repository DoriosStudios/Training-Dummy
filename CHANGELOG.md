# Training Dummy v1.1.0

This update adds a complete visual model and a clearer, always-visible global DPS display to the Training Dummy.

## HIGHLIGHTS

- Added an always-visible rolling DPS display.
- Added a complete Training Dummy inventory, hotbar and hand model.
- Preserved individual floating damage numbers for every hit.

## ADDED

- Added an always-visible global DPS display using damage received during the last second.
- Added a complete Training Dummy model for the block's inventory, hotbar, and hand appearance.

## CHANGED

- Simplified DPS tracking to one shared rolling value per Training Dummy.
- Preserved floating damage numbers for every individual hit.

## FIXED

- Prevented the DPS nametag from disappearing while the dummy is idle.
